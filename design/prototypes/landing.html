Reliability

Kill a worker. Watch nothing break.

This is the guarantee the whole system is built around. A worker can die at any point, mid-send, with no cleanup — and the campaign still finishes with exactly one send per recipient.

Why it holds

Every job is claimed with a time-boxed lease, not a permanent assignment. If the worker holding it disappears, the lease simply runs out — no signal from the dead worker is required.

A background reaper watches for expired leases and returns those jobs to pending. Whichever worker picks a job up next sends under the same idempotency key the first attempt would have used — so if the dead worker's request actually reached the provider, the retry is recognized and discarded, not duplicated.

Under the hood

Built for what happens when things go wrong.

Most email tools are designed for the happy path. Sendrun is designed for the rest of it.

Independent jobs

Every recipient is its own job with its own state, retry count, and backoff schedule. One failure never touches another recipient's send.

Idempotent sends

Every send carries an idempotency key. A retry, a webhook replay, or a re-picked job can never turn into a duplicate email.

Crash recovery

Leases expire automatically. A reaper returns orphaned jobs to pending so another worker finishes what a dead one started.

Webhook reconciliation

Provider events drive true delivery state. Duplicate and out-of-order events are deduplicated and applied by precedence rank.

AI preflight

Links, personalization variables, and spam risk are checked before launch. It flags and recommends — a person always approves.

ML recipient intelligence

Bounce risk and engagement are predicted per recipient from send history, so you know who's at risk before you send.
